About UniVibe

UniVibe is a simple platform designed to make society recruitment easier for students. Instead of searching through different posters, messages and social media pages, students can explore societies in one place and find communities that match their interests.

Whether you're into coding, music, sports or social work — there's something here for you. Browse around, take the quiz, and find your people.
